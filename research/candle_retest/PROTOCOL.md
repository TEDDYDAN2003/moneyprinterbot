# Candle/retest challenger — locked rules for this comparison

Question: does a *second* affordable visit to a previously confirmed M1/M5/M15 zone, not a market entry after the rejection, leave room for a real candle stop? Use 0.09 XAUUSD lots, 100 oz/lot, $10 reference TP, $5 **maximum modeled risk per leg**, one leg, real spread, zero added slippage/commission, 0.300 maximum spread. Do not widen a stop, resize or relax a spread filter to obtain fills.

Freeze before evaluating returns:

- Completed M1 reaction candle plus its immediate predecessor; source-TF patterns are additional logged context only. Favor a bullish/bearish real-body engulf or a lower/upper rejection wick in the trade direction. A confirming directional close after the preceding candle showed that pattern **at this same zone** also qualifies. Dojis/inside bars alone are not directional signals. Pattern is necessary in the filtered variants, never sufficient without a zone reaction.
- At most one pending setup per symbol. After confirmation, bid must return **inside the frozen band** within 180 seconds. Cancel on an opposite structure break/zone signal, lost data continuity, elapsed TTL, a tick through invalidation, or objective already reached. Do not enter retrospectively at the wick price. Sell stop: above the bullish-side stops' sweep/reaction high; buy stop: below the opposite low. Take the farther of the actual reaction wick and already-known structural invalidation; buffer one price tick outward. On a bid chart add entry spread for an ask-triggered short stop. No stop loosening. Only enter on an executable quote that fits the cash budget.
- Compare: (A) prior immediate/fixed zone rule; (B) affordable return, fixed cash SL (same wick-affordability admission); (C) same return, actual wick SL; (D) add directional candle confirmation to C; (E) D with a static next-zone target of at least $10; (F) E plus adverse completed-candle exits and quote-level profit protection. This is a ladder of comparisons, not a parameter search. Active exits: opposite BOS with adverse pressure, opposite engulf/rejection with adverse pressure, or two consecutive strong adverse M1 momentum readings (0.30). Once marked profit reaches $10, exit on a 40% giveback from sampled peak. Broker hard SL/TP remains attached. No extrapolated profit from shaking off a losing trade.
- Decision after bar close; structural invalidation and attached barriers use actual ordered ticks. For entry and discretionary quote management, sample the first actual tick in each UTC two-second bucket, approximating the monitor's polling, not retrospective best ticks. Quote/latency parity with a live terminal is not assumed. No entry on a definitely invalidated setup even if the price recovers between polls. SL/TP at touched barriers in the primary zero-slippage model. Keep standard session and portfolio guards; reset account separately per window. Report trades, wins, PF, expectancy, realized DD, risk per entry and reasons. No score from an empty sample.
- Independent public feed, not XAUUSDm: Sept 3–30 (fresh historical replication); Oct 1–28 and Oct 29–Nov 11 (previously examined windows, same controls); Nov 12–15 (new, short forward-dated slice). No model fitting. Report all windows and variants, including nulls or losses. Additional research-only paired diagnostic: freeze actual original immediate/fixed entries, evaluate the same timestamp/direction/lot/$10 TP with the wick stop even when wider than $5. Report dollar exposure and net, not a deployable account curve or a win-rate-only comparison. Do not promote on these four windows alone.
