# Causal market-structure method for the paired stop study

## Purpose and boundaries

This research-only component supplies stop candidates and descriptive structure state. It does not modify production code, generate BUY/SELL signals, filter frozen entries, retrain a model, or change the original signal decisions. The reference Pine design supplies the concepts (classified swings, close-confirmed BOS, protected levels, and MSS/CHOCH); its Wickless entry condition is not used.

The frozen signal baseline is the prior experimental runner's final selected-trade log. The baseline was generated by side-conditioned `p_long`/`p_short` classifiers trained on fixed price TP/SL first-passage labels; the simulator selected `argmax(p_long, p_short)` and required the maximum probability to be at least 0.30. Exact executed timestamps, selected side, recorded maximum probability, timeframe, lots, session/context labels, and old exit outcome are retained in `results/frozen_baseline_entries.csv`. The raw pair of probabilities was not written to that trade log, so only the executed side and selected probability can be frozen from the available artifact. This is an experimental, final-holdout trade stream, not a production signal or complete candidate-signal log.

## Swing identification and availability

The engine uses an ATR-scaled directional-change swing method, independently on completed M1, M5, and M15 OHLC bars. It does not impose the Pine asset's 3/10 settings or another fixed left/right pivot count.

1. Compute true range and a 14-bar simple rolling mean of true range on that timeframe (14 complete bars required), matching the prior research runner's ATR convention.
2. Track a running candidate high during an upswing or candidate low during a downswing. At each newly completed candle, update the candidate extreme and freeze the ATR value observed when that extreme was set.
3. Confirm a candidate high only when a later completed candle **closes** at least `k × frozen ATR14` below it. Confirm a candidate low only when a later completed candle closes at least that threshold above it. The primary, predeclared setting is `k=1.0`.
4. Store both the extreme's timestamp and its later confirmation timestamp. The swing is unavailable to the strategy until the confirmation close. No state is backdated to the candidate extreme.
5. Compare confirmed highs with the prior confirmed high (`HH` / `LH`) and confirmed lows with the prior confirmed low (`HL` / `LL`). Initialization waits for sufficient ATR and a first confirmed reversal; no prehistory structure is invented.

This is a causal, volatility-normalized OHLC approximation because the reference's plotted pivot/zigzag implementation does not specify a single causal confirmation formula that can be reproduced exactly. It is not claimed to be pixel-identical to the Pine display. Its `k=1` choice is fixed for this stop experiment; sensitivity values must be a later, separately predeclared research study, never selected from these holdout results.

## BOS, protected levels, and MSS/CHOCH

- A confirmed swing high/low arms a corresponding structural break level. Only a **strict completed close** above the high emits bullish BOS, and only a strict completed close below the low emits bearish BOS. A wick alone does not qualify. A consumed level is not re-armed until a new confirmed swing of that type forms.
- While a confirmed high is awaiting bullish BOS, the protected-low candidate is initialized from the lowest low between the pivot extreme and its confirmation close (those bars are already completed and known when confirmation arrives), then updated through the BOS close. On bullish BOS, that pullback low becomes the protected low. Symmetrically, the highest high from a confirmed swing-low extreme through bearish BOS becomes the protected high.
- In bullish state, a completed close below protected low emits bearish MSS/CHOCH; in bearish state, a close above protected high emits bullish MSS/CHOCH. A reversal updates the opposite protected level from the preceding directional leg extreme and changes trend only at that close.
- Structure state and events preserve timestamps, swing class, BOS/MSS direction, levels, trend, and the current protected high/low. Each timeframe has independent state. A state is joined to an entry only at the latest timeframe close `<=` the recorded next-M1-open entry timestamp.
- Range compression (`20` completed bars with range `< 3.5 × ATR14`) and mixed recent swing classes are descriptive labels only. They do not alter entries.

## Stop candidates

At each frozen entry, the study records four same-entry stop prices:

1. Existing fixed stop: entry ± $0.75 price units.
2. Latest confirmed same-timeframe swing low for BUY / swing high for SELL.
3. Latest valid protected low for BUY / protected high for SELL.
4. Protected level with a 0.10 × entry-timeframe ATR14 buffer beyond the level.

Missing or wrong-side structural levels are unavailable; the entry is excluded from **all** variants in the paired cohort. No stop is silently replaced. The same original lot size, $1.50 TP, next-M1-open fill, horizon, and transaction-cost equation apply to every variant. The preserved lots mean cash risk differs across stops; that is intentional to keep position sizing identical in the paired stop comparison. The follow-up experiment can later compare a common sizing policy.

## Validation before stop use

`structure_engine.validate_causality_and_semantics()` is a mandatory runner gate. Its deterministic OHLC fixture verifies:

- swing records appear only at a later confirmation close (separate from their pivot timestamp);
- bullish and bearish BOS transitions;
- protected level emitted with BOS;
- bearish and bullish MSS transitions;
- a wick-only cross cannot emit BOS;
- all event times are no earlier than their recorded pivot extremes.

The study also writes per-close structure state Parquet and event CSVs for all three timeframes. Entry-state joins use a backward as-of timestamp lookup, so no later timeframe close is available at an earlier decision. The engine is then used to create stops only after all synthetic checks pass.

## Data, execution and inferential limits

The source archive is M1 OHLC with broker tick-volume and spread-point fields; there is no tick tape, historical bid/ask path, or nonzero real volume. M5/M15 bars are formed from complete M1 bars using the previous runner's close-time convention. Execution stays at the original simulator's next M1 open, fixed spread floor $0.50, $0.10 slippage per side, $7 round-turn commission per lot, and 100 oz per lot. Cost is modeled as $0.77 price-equivalent per lot unit. If one M1 bar touches TP and SL, stop is assumed first. Gaps in the complete maximum holding horizon are excluded from every variant. The paired cohort also excludes later entries that would overlap the longest exit among its stop variants.

The prior classifier's fixed-barrier target is a material mismatch: its probabilities do **not** estimate TP-before-structural-SL. Paired stop results assess the old executed decisions under alternative stop placements; they do not establish calibrated structural-stop probabilities or a viable new entry model. The saved prior final period was already inspected during earlier research, so this is a paired diagnostic on that period, not an untouched fresh holdout. Small sample sizes and OHLC path uncertainty limit inference. No result authorizes paper/live execution.
