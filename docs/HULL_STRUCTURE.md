# Hull entry rule: protected swing v2

**Protect the swing that produced the break, not each small swing inside its retest.** The Python scanner, observer and ordered-quote replay identify this policy as `protected_swing_v2`. The [Pine study](../indicators/FC-Hull-Either-16-Structure.pine) and [MT5 indicator](../MT5/INSTALL-MT5.md) use the same structure on their own native candles. A closed chart suggestion is not a broker ticket. Compare **the same OHLC, initial history, settings and candle-open UTC timestamp**; ICE and Exness can legitimately differ.

**Scanner timing is not chart timing.** With the default `live_continuation_v1`, if the trend and pullback were already established on closed candles, the Python scanner can act on the first observed matching Hull cross **during** the next forming candle at the broker quote. The candle does not get to certify its own structure. CHoCH, the post-BOS countertrend exception, and opposite-label exits are still closed decisions. Pine/MT5 arrows and `hull-replay` remain **closed-label benchmarks**, not a record of live crosses or fills.

## Three distinct moments

| Event | Bearish case | Bullish mirror |
|---|---|---|
| **CHoCH: direction changes** | A close strictly below the protected bullish HL. A fresh SELL on **that very close** may qualify. | Strict close above the protected bearish LH; a fresh BUY may qualify immediately. |
| **Pullback: continuation entry can qualify** | After a leg low, a **later completed candle closes higher than the preceding close**. A fresh SELL may qualify strictly **below the protected LH**. | After a leg high, a later candle closes lower. A fresh BUY may qualify strictly **above the protected HL**. |
| **BOS: the next leg proves itself** | With a prior pullback, a strict close below the previous **confirmed structural LL** is BOS. Only now may the pullback high become the protected LH. The next confirmed LL must be lower. | Strict close above the previous confirmed HH after a pullback; promote the intervening pullback low. The next HH must be higher. |

There is **no 50% gate on trend entries**, no wait for three right bars on the *retest*, and no wait for the *next* BOS to admit the preceding pullback entry. A raw Hull cross is still mandatory: structure alone is never an order. On a CHoCH signal, the executable quote must remain strictly on the breakout side of the **broken** protected level. For a trend continuation, it must remain strictly on the trend side of the **protected** swing in the frozen signal snapshot. A BOS close itself may qualify from the already-seen pullback; the new leg does not inherit that permission on later candles.

**Separate countertrend exception, unchanged:** after bullish continuation BOS **and a confirmed new HH**, a fresh SELL may qualify only at `old broken HH < price ≤ new HH`. After bearish continuation BOS **and a confirmed new LL**, a fresh BUY may qualify only at `new LL ≤ price < old broken LL`. A fresh CHoCH alone never opens this opposite-direction exception.

## Exactly what a closed bar knows

1. Local pivots still use the configured left/right confirmation (default 3/3; newest equal wick wins). The first strict close through a previously known pivot, with an opposing pivot available, **seeds context only**. A subsequent real CHoCH or proven continuation BOS establishes a tradable leg. Each symbol/timeframe has its own state.
2. Test the **old protected level** and previous structural extreme before processing today's wicks. A wick through the guard, an equal close, or a close through only an internal bounce does not reverse the bias. At a genuine CHoCH the new guard is the outermost wick of the preceding leg through the **previous** candle, not the latest minor pivot. No assumed intrabar ordering.
3. Track the emerging tip. A candle which sets a new/equal unconfirmed tip cannot be its own pullback. A later strict counter-direction close is sufficient evidence, including on the signal close; a flat close or just a wick is not. A new unconfirmed tip resets that evidence for the new leg low/high. No past label is revised. A structural HH/LL is only published for a confirmed swing **strictly beyond** the broken level.
4. Through a pullback the protected guard is frozen. On continuation BOS consider the **deepest wick across the whole intervening pullback**, from after the tip through the **previous** candle, not the first or latest small pivot or the break candle's other wick. Promote it only if it is a strictly higher low / lower high between the old guard and the extreme. A wick sweep or equal old guard cannot loosen/replace protection: the old guard survives that BOS. No prior pullback means a same-leg extension, not a new BOS.
5. The chart evaluates each cross at its close; the live scanner does not re-enter on a confirmed continuation it was watching intrabar. Same-side repeats while occupied, a busy account, bad stops, a stale quote, a rejected price or other execution gates can still block an order. Future candles never rescue a rejected cross. See [flat timing and protection](HULL_DEMO.md#what-triggers-an-entry).

## Reproducible gold-shaped regression — synthetic, not your trading history

In the deterministic `protected-sell` fixture, **3,682.840** is the LH whose downswing broke the previous low. The first small pullback peaks at **3,675.945**. The next rally closes above that minor high but below 3,682.840. Bar 92 closes at **3,677.159** with a fresh Hull SELL. On these same synthetic candles v1 flips bullish and vetoes it; v2 keeps the bearish guard, reports `bear_protected_pullback_sell` and passes the close. Only a later close below the structural LL **3,670.260** promotes the intervening high **3,679.830**. The earlier SELL passes even when the input stops at bar 92. Tests cover the BUY mirror, a fresh signal on the true CHoCH close, and a next-open touch that vetoes a fill without erasing its suggestion. The reported incident prices are anchors, **not a reconstruction of the broker's full history**.

To inspect a broker candidate, find `live_hull_cross` or `closed_label` for **symbol + timeframe + UTC trigger**. Distinguish the protected level from the tentative wick and `label` (raw) from `entry_side` (qualified). On charts use the Data Window fields for **protected HL/LH, confirmed extreme, candidate wick, bias, signed break on this close and pullback seen**. A one-sided trend rule has one displayed boundary / one null price bound; a two-sided countertrend band has both edges.

## Orders, exits and audit

- Check the live signal bid or required closing candle first; recheck the **same frozen band** on each proposed BUY **ask** or SELL **bid**, including subsequent legs and pre-send rechecks. A batch still needs the existing spread, stop, target, age, session, exposure, margin and arming checks. A broker fill may differ from the quote. The **initial** order stop still uses the configured stop method (default: previous local wicks), **not** the full structural range. After a fill the independent [profit ladder](HULL_DEMO.md#profit-ladder-for-new-tickets) may tighten it; never use a projected profit lock to pass initial risk limits.
- At the latest actual trigger (not the future end of a forming bar), aligned **entry-qualified** lanes use the highest timeframe; opposing **qualified** lanes skip. An invalid lane cannot override a valid entry. If no lane qualifies, do not fall back to an earlier close.
- **`exit_opposite` is unchanged.** The independent **closed-label** pre-structure selector still determines whether to request an exit on owned opposite positions, independently of opening a replacement. An invalid entry is not a new exit rule or an exemption from the configured one. A raw conflict still produces no selected exit. Busy-symbol checks prevent opening into an unresolved position. Replay now models that configured exit on the first executable bid/ask for the held side.
- Read `closed_label` or `live_hull_cross` for the raw `label`, pre-structure `side`/`reason`, `structure` snapshot, `entry_setup`, `protected_level`, `pullback_candidate`, `pullback_seen_close_s`, `zone_low`/`zone_high` and their `_open` flags, `entry_side` and `entry_reason`. **`entry_side = 0` means no entry suggestion.** `entry_side = ±1` means qualified at the recorded trigger, not filled. `label_decision.outcome` reports later vetoes or the batch result. `structure` records pivot `bar_s` (open), `known_s` (observation/confirmation close), and `break_close_s`, all Unix UTC seconds.
- For a **closed** signal, a temporarily missing/pre-close/aged first tick is **not** a failed price-band test. See [the entry timeline](HULL_DEMO.md#how-to-read-an-entry-timeline). During this bounded wait only, if another native lane reports the same close, the same qualified-direction / highest-timeframe rule applies before the first usable quote. An invalid or duplicate lane does not erase the entry, and no lane extends the deadline. A conflict, hard veto or completed attempt is final.
- Replay reports `label_counts`, `entry_qualified_at_close` and `structure_vetoes_at_close` separately from positions/fills. [Original structure check under its recorded limits](../research/hull_labels/structure_check/summary.json) · [raw and qualified labels](../research/hull_labels/structure_check/labels.csv). Those files are historical v1 evidence, not v2 results or a profitability estimate.
