# Dynamic Hull · paired market entries on ticks and fresh closes

The shipped `config.yaml` is **paper**. Its `entry_timing: by_route` selects **`intrabar_cross`** for paired market orders. The default parent → trigger pairs are **M5 → M3, M10 → M5, M15 → M10**. An example using M5 → M4 does not change that mapping. The chart indicators, Wickless, lot sizes, rate, sessions and risk limits are unchanged.

## A trade, in order

1. **Confirm structure, not the trigger candle.** The last closed parent and last closed trigger must both agree with the trade. Within the forming child candle, the bot projects the actual Dynamic Hull lines on the current Bid price. A BUY needs B strictly above the selected A after B was strictly below that same A at the preceding close. Reverse for SELL. Switching from the High A to the Low A is not a crossover. A held signal on attachment, an ambiguous tick or missing data is not a new entry.
2. **Use the child’s range swing.** For a BUY use the last still-valid, confirmed **HL** of the child’s bullish range. Within a pullback the structure indicator keeps the lowest classified swing low. For a SELL it keeps the highest classified swing high (**LH**) in the bearish range. Only pivots already confirmed by the configured `swing_len` at the time of the tick qualify. The stop is not the parent guard, an N-bar extreme or the unfinished cross candle’s wick. No qualifying swing, or one on the wrong side of current Bid? Skip.
3. **Freeze the intersection, not the fill.** Use the actual intersection of the two selected Hull lines and the trigger candle’s **Bid low and high as known at that tick** (including both wicks and their endpoints). Never reclassify it using the final range. The BUY trigger Bid must be strictly above the cross; SELL strictly below. Equality or missing evidence blocks entry. The confirmed swing plus its usual outward cushion remains the base stop; never loosen it.
4. **If the cross is outside that range**, attach the tighter of the base stop and the actual intersection on the original order. **If inside**, attach the normal swing SL and TP and wait for that exact candle’s final Bid close. While waiting, a live uncross against the original source still requests immediate exit. If the lines hold, SELL must close **below** the frozen cross; BUY **above**. A touch or a close on the wrong side reserves exits on those verified tickets, even if the next quote has recovered. A favorable close requests a tighten-only SL, preserving TP and any stronger protection. The broker SL is rounded outward to the tick grid; a new SELL chart stop is translated to an Ask-triggered level using the executable spread. The fill is not the stop. There is no periodic widening for spread.
5. **Check each leg independently.** A later tick can move the projected intersection without changing the original cross. The same candle, source, uninterrupted episode, aligned closed structures, original swing and both price-side checks must still be verifiable. Repeated newer ticks can move several trade ticks or retrace without invalidating the level. Discard only the **unsent** request, price the remaining size on the newly verified Bid/Ask, and repeat the exposure, funding and broker checks. A wider but permitted spread is revalued for that new leg, never applied to an installed stop. The original batch count, leg IDs and deadline do not reset. A price-only refusal at `order_check` can use a genuinely changed quote; an uncertain `order_send`, partial fill or other veto never authorizes a resend or later top-up. If a quote advances between child/parent reads, both the old prefix and the new stable pair must be provable; unexplained wicks, missing data or an ambiguous timestamp stop the batch. A new same-candle recross needs a distinct later tick **after verified flat**. All existing skip-after-MSS, session, age and exposure checks still apply.
6. **A separately confirmed cross can enter.** When there was no consumed intrabar entry opportunity on that close, the default paired market runner arbitrates the actual native closed label. It uses the closed candle’s wick stop tightened by its intersection, not a guessed intrabar swing. Both streams and the successor quote must still verify. A reserved or skipped live origin is not traded again on close. If an independent actionable tick and a different actionable close arrive together, skip the conflict rather than send two batches. Within a tick or a co-close group the normal highest-parent/same-side and opposite-side veto rules apply.
7. **An eligible close can wait briefly for verified flatness.** Every remaining opposing position must be identified and already have its own valid exit intent. An acknowledgement or `matched_positions: 0` is not a flat snapshot. After reconciliation, repeat the quote, context, session, spread and funding checks. The deadline stays **signal close + configured maximum entry age** (template: 8 seconds), not eight seconds after flat. No queue survives a restart. An unrelated manual/foreign ticket is never flattened to make room.

**Example:** A SELL triggers at Bid 2002.40 with the frozen cross at 2005.19 inside its candle. Keep the swing SL attached. If the exact candle closes at 2005.20, request exit; if it closes at 2004.90 with the same Hull relation intact, tighten toward 2005.19 without moving a stronger SL. Actual rounding, spread and broker distance still apply. If a separate fresh closed SELL arrives at 12:44:00 while owned longs are closing, it can enter after verified flat at 12:44:02 if all checks pass; **not** at 12:44:09 under the default limit.

## Choose timing without changing other settings

| Configuration | What happens |
|---|---|
| `paired_structure` + `market_next_quote` + `by_route` (shipped), or explicit `intrabar_cross` | Tick entries plus the separately confirmed fallback above |
| Explicit `confirmed_cross_next_bar`, or older private YAML with timing omitted | Retained closed-cross policy, including its wick stop |
| `direct_retest_limit` + `by_route` or `confirmed_cross_next_bar` | Retained **closed-candle** limit policy; never staged from a forming cross |
| M1 / parent-close + `by_route` | Original confirmed entry clock |

An explicit `intrabar_cross` with retest limits or a non-paired mode is rejected. `--paired-confirm` selects the paired execution book; it does not change timing, add a pair or authorize orders. An ignored `config.demo.local.yaml` is a **full config**: a code update does not edit it. For intrabar market timing, while flat with no pending/unknown orders, set `hull.entry_timing: by_route` (or `intrabar_cross`) and keep `hull.entry_route: market_next_quote`, `hull.execution_mode: paired_structure` and your existing authorized pairs. Do not copy the template over local sizes, symbols, sessions or caps. Do not switch managers or delete state to replay an entry.

- **Offline validation:** `python -m bot.cli validate-config --config config.yaml` needs no MT5. The resolved timing is shown in scanner/preflight output, not inferred from a chart arrow.
- **Read-only verification on the intended Windows demo host:** use `hull-preflight` with the intended YAML/profiles. Look for `entry_timing: intrabar_cross`, `entry_policy: intrabar_pair_structure_cross_v1`, `entry_quote_policy: first_verified_cross_tick_v1`, `new_ticket_stop_policy: confirmed_trigger_range_hl_lh_v1`, `stop_spread_policy: sell_ask_entry_only_exact_risk_v2`, `closed_market_fallback: fresh_unconsumed_confirmed_pair_after_verified_flat`, `intrabar_cross_range_basis: frozen_trigger_bid_wick_range_v2`, the expected `parent_to_trigger` and `pair_history_ready: true`. An empty `last_confirmed_trigger_swing` is not permission to invent one. Observation and tests do not authorize arming.

## Audit and limits

`intrabar_arbitration` records the tick and competing pairs. `intrabar_pair_cross` records the selected trigger, swing and structure; `label_decision` reports the result. `demo_result.unsent_reprices` counts discarded pre-send requests for that leg. `demo_result` separates `trigger_msc` from `send_tick_time_msc`, `trigger_to_submit_ms`, the requested broker stop and the returned deal ID. New tags are `q<parent>x<child>_<trigger-ms>_<leg>`. The ledger keeps distinct same-second recrosses separate, leaves `signal_close_utc` and the historical wick fields empty, and attaches swing/quote evidence only to matching broker fills. `origin_cross_check`, `exit_request` and `lane_exit_confirmed` are **an observation, a request and a reconciliation**, not three fills.

`cross_region_location` and `cross_signal_low` / `cross_signal_high` in the tick receipt describe the frozen range. `cross_region_stop_activation` records deferred or confirmed stop changes; `exit_pending` is not a fill. An uncertain amendment is reconciled against the **sent SL and TP** on the same ticket, not against a new spread. The ladder’s rung and original risk basis do not change. `pair_arbitration` and `p…` receipts identify the closed route; `pair_wait_opposite_flat` includes the original deadline.

This is a polling scanner, not an exchange tick subscription. A move that appears and disappears between observations cannot be reconstructed from final OHLC. A restart does not replay entries. If the terminal cannot supply the exact originating close, the manager logs `origin_close_unverified`, retains broker protection and retries that candle; it does not guess from a later label. Broker rejection, disconnection or a slow synchronous call can delay or prevent an exit. Tests use synthetic candles and broker responses, not live prices or profitability estimates.
