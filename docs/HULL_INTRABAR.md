# Dynamic Hull · paired market entries before the candle closes

The shipped `config.yaml` is **paper**. Its `entry_timing: by_route` selects **`intrabar_cross`** for paired market orders. The default parent → trigger pairs are **M5 → M3, M10 → M5, M15 → M10**. An example using M5 → M4 does not change that mapping. The chart indicators, Wickless, lot sizes, rate, sessions and risk limits are unchanged.

## A trade, in order

1. **Confirm structure, not the trigger candle.** The last closed parent and last closed trigger must both agree with the trade. Within the forming child candle, the bot projects the actual Dynamic Hull lines on the current Bid price. A BUY needs B strictly above the selected A after B was strictly below that same A at the preceding close. Reverse for SELL. Switching from the High A to the Low A is not a crossover. A held signal on attachment, an ambiguous tick or missing data is not a new entry.
2. **Use the child’s range swing.** For a BUY use the last still-valid, confirmed **HL** of the child’s bullish range. Within a pullback the structure indicator keeps the lowest classified swing low. For a SELL it keeps the highest classified swing high (**LH**) in the bearish range. Only pivots already confirmed by the configured `swing_len` at the time of the tick qualify. The stop is not the parent guard, an N-bar extreme or the unfinished cross candle’s wick. No qualifying swing, or one on the wrong side of current Bid? Skip.
3. **Freeze the stop before sending.** Use the child’s last closed ATR. The cushion outside the swing is the larger of one broker trade tick and `atr_buffer × ATR + extra_pips × pip_size`, rounded outward. BUY uses this raw Bid-chart SL. SELL translates it to an Ask-triggered broker SL using the entry spread and an outward tick rounding. The initial broker stop, not the raw swing, determines planned risk. No spread widening after entry.
4. **Act on the first verified cross tick**, not on the next bar. BUY at the verified Ask, SELL at the verified Bid, subject to the existing desk/broker checks. Each leg must still be in the same cross episode, inside both the entry-age limit and the original candle. A missed opportunity is not parked until close. At one tick, opposite eligible pairs skip; same-side ties choose the highest eligible parent. No stacking, lower-parent fallback or partial-batch top-up. The parent’s configured first-cross skip and failed-counter-MSS exception still apply.
5. **Watch the exact trade that opened.** On its originating candle (including the final print), a BUY loses its cross if B is **at or below its original A**. A SELL loses it if B is **at or above its original A**. Latch the exit for those verified tickets before waiting for a broker acknowledgement. A verified opposing child or parent cross, a confirmed parent MSS, broker SL/TP and existing per-ticket management still work independently of entry filters. A cross that holds at the close keeps its position; a zero label on the next candle is **not** an uncross.
6. **Re-entry needs a separate edge after flat.** A fresh same-candle recross on a later distinct tick can start a new batch only after the prior exposure is verified flat and all normal checks pass again. A held label cannot re-enter; no top-up after a rejection, stale tick or unknown acknowledgement. Unresolved fills remain on manual hold. The manager reuses the recorded ticket identity on restart, not a similar broker comment.

**Example:** At 11:37:12.412, an M3 BUY can use the live 11:36–11:39 M3 candle, bullish M3 structure closed at 11:36, bullish M5 structure closed at 11:35 and an already-confirmed M3 HL below Bid. If the lines meet at 11:37:12.650, request an exit then. A fresh cross at 11:37:12.900 is eligible only if the earlier batch was already verified flat. There is no 11:39 market entry just because a label also closes there. With an explicitly selected M4 trigger the same rules use **M4’s HL**, not M5’s.

## Choose timing without changing other settings

| Configuration | What happens |
|---|---|
| `paired_structure` + `market_next_quote` + `by_route` (shipped), or explicit `intrabar_cross` | Intrabar market policy above |
| Explicit `confirmed_cross_next_bar`, or older private YAML with timing omitted | Retained closed-cross policy, including its wick stop |
| `direct_retest_limit` + `by_route` or `confirmed_cross_next_bar` | Retained **closed-candle** limit policy; never staged from a forming cross |
| M1 / parent-close + `by_route` | Original confirmed entry clock |

An explicit `intrabar_cross` with retest limits or a non-paired mode is rejected. `--paired-confirm` selects the paired execution book; it does not change timing, add a pair or authorize orders. An ignored `config.demo.local.yaml` is a **full config**: a code update does not edit it. For intrabar market timing, while flat with no pending/unknown orders, set `hull.entry_timing: by_route` (or `intrabar_cross`) and keep `hull.entry_route: market_next_quote`, `hull.execution_mode: paired_structure` and your existing authorized pairs. Do not copy the template over local sizes, symbols, sessions or caps. Do not switch managers or delete state to replay an entry.

- **Offline validation:** `python -m bot.cli validate-config --config config.yaml` needs no MT5. The resolved timing is shown in scanner/preflight output, not inferred from a chart arrow.
- **Read-only verification on the intended Windows demo host:** use `hull-preflight` with the intended YAML/profiles. Look for `entry_timing: intrabar_cross`, `entry_policy: intrabar_pair_structure_cross_v1`, `entry_quote_policy: first_verified_cross_tick_v1`, `new_ticket_stop_policy: confirmed_trigger_range_hl_lh_v1`, `stop_spread_policy: sell_ask_entry_only_exact_risk_v2`, the expected `parent_to_trigger` and `pair_history_ready: true`. An empty `last_confirmed_trigger_swing` is not permission to invent one. Observation and tests do not authorize arming.

## Audit and limits

`intrabar_arbitration` records the tick and competing pairs. `intrabar_pair_cross` records the selected trigger, swing and structure; `label_decision` reports the result. `demo_result` separates `trigger_msc` from `send_tick_time_msc`, `trigger_to_submit_ms`, the requested broker stop and the returned deal ID. New tags are `q<parent>x<child>_<trigger-ms>_<leg>`. The ledger keeps distinct same-second recrosses separate, leaves `signal_close_utc` and the historical wick fields empty, and attaches swing/quote evidence only to matching broker fills. `origin_cross_check`, `exit_request` and `lane_exit_confirmed` are **an observation, a request and a reconciliation**, not three fills.

This is a polling scanner, not an exchange tick subscription. A move that appears and disappears between observations cannot be reconstructed from final OHLC. A restart does not replay entries. If the terminal cannot supply the exact originating close, the manager logs `origin_close_unverified`, retains broker protection and retries that candle; it does not guess from a later label. Broker rejection, disconnection or a slow synchronous call can delay or prevent an exit. Tests use synthetic candles and broker responses, not live prices or profitability estimates.
