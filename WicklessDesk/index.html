Field manual  /  Strategy 015

        
What counts as a trade?

        

          

            01 / IDENTIFY
            
Find the wickless side.

            

              On a completed M15 bar: bullish open at the low for BUY; bearish
              open at the high for SELL. The opposite wick is allowed. A doji
              never qualifies.
            

          

          

            02 / WAIT
            
Let price retest.

            

              A later M15 candle must overlap the zone and close in the trade
              direction. It may finish inside the zone. The 3-bar internal
              structure trend must still agree.
            

          

          

            03 / PRICE
            
Measure one risk unit.

            

              Freeze the structural stop at setup. Place the target the same
              distance from entry: BUY at 102 with SL 98 gives TP 106. The MT5
              fill can differ from the chart close.
            

          

        

        

          Zone 100–103: a BUY is invalid below 100; a SELL is
          invalid above 103. A wick through is not enough by itself, unless it
          reaches the frozen stop. Invalidation wins over a touch on the same
          candle.
        

        

          No Hull crosses, resting limits, countertrend trades or trailing
          ladder. Flat and retest-window checks govern the separate demo runner;
          this desk configures it, not your broker account.
